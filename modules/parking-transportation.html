Event Parking Planning Guide

Small Events (Under 50 attendees)

  • Direct attendees to nearest general parking lot or garage
  • Provide clear parking instructions in event communications
  • Consider virtual parking permits for daytime events
  • Evening/weekend events may not require special permits

Medium Events (50-200 attendees)

  • Contact Parking Services at least 2 weeks in advance
  • Request reserved parking area if available
  • Arrange for visitor parking permits
  • Consider shuttle service from remote lots if needed
  • Post parking attendants or signage at lot entrances

Large Events (200+ attendees)

  • Contact Parking Services 4-6 weeks in advance
  • Request dedicated parking lot(s)
  • Coordinate with UCF Police for traffic management
  • Arrange shuttle buses from remote parking areas
  • Hire parking attendants for lot management
  • Create detailed parking plan with backup options

Accessible Parking

  • Ensure accessible parking near venue entrance
  • Reserve ADA-compliant spaces
  • Verify accessible pathways from parking to venue
  • Provide clear signage for accessible parking
  • Include accessible parking info in communications

Transportation Best Practices

Important Tips:

  • Communicate Early: Include detailed parking instructions in all event communications
  • Consider Timing: Peak class times (Mon-Thu 9am-6pm) have limited parking - evening events are easier
  • Athletic Game Days: Avoid scheduling during home football games - parking is extremely limited
  • Alternative Transportation: Encourage carpooling, rideshare, or public transit for large events
  • Weather Backup: Have contingency plans for rain (covered parking options, umbrellas, covered walkways)
  • Signage: Use clear directional signs from parking areas to your venue
  • Contact Information: Provide event organizer contact for attendees with parking questions

Alternative Transportation Options

Encourage Sustainable Transportation:

  • UCF Shuttles: Utilize existing campus shuttle routes when possible
  • Rideshare: Create designated rideshare pickup/dropoff zones
  • Bike Parking: Ensure bike racks are available near venue
  • SunRail: For downtown campus events, promote SunRail access
  • Carpooling: Set up carpool matching for large conferences
