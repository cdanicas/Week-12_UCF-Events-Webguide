Choosing the Right Venue

Key Factors to Consider:

  • Capacity: Choose a venue that comfortably fits your expected attendance (plan for 10-15% more than RSVP count)
  • Location: Consider accessibility, parking, and proximity to your target audience
  • Layout: Ensure the space configuration works for your event type (theater, banquet, classroom, etc.)
  • AV Equipment: Verify included technology and any additional rental needs
  • Catering: Check if venue has in-house catering or allows outside vendors
  • Setup/Breakdown: Understand time allowed for setup before and cleanup after your event
  • Accessibility: Ensure ADA compliance and accessible parking
  • Budget: Get detailed pricing including any hidden fees or required services
