Catering Best Practices

Planning Your Catering

  • Order for 10-15% more than confirmed RSVP count
  • Plan 5-7 business days ahead for orders
  • Consider time of day and event length
  • Ask about dietary restrictions in registration
  • Verify delivery time and setup requirements
  • Confirm tables, linens, and serving ware needs
  • Plan for beverage service and ice

Budget Considerations

  • Food typically costs $8-$25 per person
  • Add 15-20% for service and delivery fees
  • Beverages add $2-$5 per person
  • Consider cost-effective options like boxed lunches
  • Breakfast is generally less expensive than lunch/dinner
  • Buffet style can be more economical than plated meals
  • Get quotes from multiple vendors for comparison

Dietary Accommodations

  • Always offer vegetarian options
  • Ask about allergies and restrictions during registration
  • Common restrictions: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher, halal
  • Label all dishes with ingredients and allergens
  • Keep allergen-free items separate from other foods
  • Have vendor contact info for guest questions
  • Plan for 15-20% vegetarian, 5-10% other restrictions

Service Styles

Buffet: Self-service, good for large groups, requires serving staff

Boxed Meals: Individual portions, easy distribution, portable

Plated Service: Formal, requires service staff, higher cost

Stations: Multiple food areas, encourages mingling, interactive

Cocktail Reception: Passed hors d'oeuvres, stand-up service

Meal Planning by Event Type

Breakfast Events (7am-10am)

Continental breakfast, breakfast sandwiches, pastries, coffee service

Recommended: $8-$12 per person

Lunch Events (11am-2pm)

Boxed lunches, sandwich platters, salad bar, hot buffet

Recommended: $12-$18 per person

Dinner Events (5pm-8pm)

Hot buffet, plated meals, multiple courses

Recommended: $18-$30 per person

Snacks & Breaks

Light refreshments, cookies, fruit, beverages

Recommended: $3-$8 per person

Receptions

Hors d'oeuvres, finger foods, appetizers

Recommended: $10-$20 per person

Catering Timeline

Important Deadlines:

  • 3-4 weeks before: Review menus, get quotes, select vendor
  • 2-3 weeks before: Place order with preliminary guest count
  • 1 week before: Send registration reminders to get final RSVP count
  • 5-7 days before: Provide final guest count to caterer
  • 2-3 days before: Confirm delivery time, setup, and special requests
  • Day of: Arrive early to oversee delivery and setup
  • After event: Arrange pickup of rental items and leftover food

Catering for Other Campuses

Downtown Campus: Contact local Orlando catering vendors or coordinate with downtown venue

Lake Nona Campus: Check with Medical City area catering services

Rosen Campus: Leverage Hospitality program connections and I-Drive area restaurants

Note: Main campus UCF Dining Services primarily serves the Orlando main campus. Other locations may require external vendors.
