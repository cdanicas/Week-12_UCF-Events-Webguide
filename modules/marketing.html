Event Marketing

Promote your UCF event effectively using official brand standards and proven marketing strategies.

UCF Brand Standards

Official UCF Brand Guidelines

All UCF event materials must follow official university brand standards for logos, colors, fonts, and messaging.

Brand Standards Include:

  • UCF logo usage and placement
  • Official color palette (Black and Gold)
  • Typography guidelines (Gotham font family)
  • Photography and imagery standards
  • Messaging and tone guidelines
  • Print and digital templates

UCF Marketing Channels

Campus Channels (Free)

  • UCF Events Calendar: Submit events to events.ucf.edu
  • Knights Email: UCF email announcements (approval required)
  • Digital Signage: Campus TV displays and boards
  • UCF Social Media: Tag @UCF for potential shares
  • Student Union Bulletin Boards: Physical posting spaces
  • Department Newsletters: College/department communications
  • UCF Today: Submit news to UCF Today

Paid/Purchased Channels

  • Print Materials: Flyers, posters, banners (design and printing costs)
  • Social Media Ads: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn sponsored posts
  • Digital Ads: Google Ads, display advertising
  • Email Marketing: External email services (if needed)
  • Promotional Items: Branded giveaways and swag
  • Media Buys: Local radio, newspapers, online publications

Event Marketing Timeline

6-8 Weeks Before Event

  • Create event branding following UCF standards
  • Develop key messaging and promotional copy
  • Design graphics and visual assets
  • Set up event registration/ticketing system
  • Create event website or landing page
  • Submit to UCF Events Calendar

4-6 Weeks Before Event

  • Launch social media promotion campaign
  • Send initial email announcement
  • Distribute printed flyers/posters on campus
  • Contact media for potential coverage
  • Reach out to partner organizations for cross-promotion
  • Post on digital signage

2-4 Weeks Before Event

  • Increase social media posting frequency
  • Send reminder emails to mailing list
  • Share countdown posts
  • Highlight speakers/performers/activities
  • Launch any paid advertising campaigns
  • Engage with followers and answer questions

1-2 Weeks Before Event

  • Send final reminder emails
  • Create "last chance to register" urgency
  • Share behind-the-scenes content
  • Post event logistics (parking, what to bring, etc.)
  • Engage influencers or student ambassadors

Week of Event

  • Daily countdown posts
  • Share final logistics and reminders
  • Create excitement with sneak peeks
  • Respond promptly to last-minute questions

Day of Event

  • Live posting/tweeting during event
  • Share photos and videos in real-time
  • Encourage attendee-generated content with event hashtag
  • Thank attendees and sponsors

After Event

  • Share event highlights and photo gallery
  • Thank attendees and sponsors publicly
  • Share key takeaways or outcomes
  • Send follow-up survey
  • Post recap video or summary
  • Promote next year's event (if recurring)

Email Marketing Best Practices

Effective Event Emails Include:

  • Compelling Subject Line: Clear, concise, creates urgency
  • Eye-Catching Header: Event name, date, UCF branding
  • Clear CTA: "Register Now" button prominently displayed
  • Key Details: Date, time, location, what to expect
  • Benefits: Why attendees should come
  • Visual Elements: Photos, graphics, following brand standards
  • Social Sharing: Easy sharing buttons
  • Contact Info: How to get questions answered

Email Sequence:

  1. Save the Date: Early announcement (6-8 weeks out)
  2. Registration Open: Formal invitation with details (4-6 weeks out)
  3. Mid-Campaign Reminder: Highlight speakers/activities (2-3 weeks out)
  4. Final Reminder: Last chance to register (1 week out)
  5. Day-Before Reminder: Logistics and what to bring (1 day before)
  6. Thank You: Post-event follow-up and survey (1-3 days after)

Marketing Materials Design

Design Checklist:

  • ✓ Uses official UCF logos (from brand.ucf.edu)
  • ✓ Follows UCF color palette (Black #000000, Gold #FFC904)
  • ✓ Uses approved fonts (Gotham or alternatives)
  • ✓ Includes essential event information (5 W's)
  • ✓ Has clear call-to-action
  • ✓ Includes registration link or QR code
  • ✓ Contact information for questions
  • ✓ Accessible (high contrast, readable fonts)
  • ✓ Consistent branding across all materials
