Event Security

Ensure your event is safe and secure with proper planning and UCF Police Department support.

UCF Police Department - Special Event Requests

Submit your special event security request to the UCF Police Department for officer presence, traffic control, and emergency planning assistance.

When to Request: At least 2-4 weeks before your event (earlier for large events)

What's Included: Uniformed officer presence, crowd management, emergency response coordination

Does Your Event Need Security?

Events That Typically Require Security

  • Events with 200+ attendees
  • Events serving alcohol
  • Events with high-profile speakers or guests
  • Late-night events (after 10pm)
  • Outdoor events in high-traffic areas
  • Events involving minors
  • Events with valuable equipment or displays
  • Controversial or politically sensitive events

Events That May Not Need Security

  • Small meetings (under 50 people)
  • Daytime classroom or conference events
  • Academic lectures in secure buildings
  • Closed events with controlled access
  • Virtual/hybrid events

Note: Even if not required, security can provide peace of mind and professional support.

Event Security Best Practices

Planning Phase (4-6 weeks before)

  • Conduct risk assessment for your event type and size
  • Submit special event request to UCF Police
  • Identify emergency exits and rally points
  • Create emergency contact list
  • Review venue security features and procedures
  • Determine if bag checks or metal detectors are needed

Setup Phase (Day before/Day of)

  • Meet with assigned security personnel
  • Walk through the venue and identify concerns
  • Set up check-in/registration procedures
  • Post emergency contact numbers
  • Test communication systems (radios, phones)
  • Mark emergency exits clearly
  • Designate staff for specific security roles

During Event

  • Monitor crowd size and behavior
  • Keep emergency lanes clear
  • Maintain communication with security team
  • Document any incidents immediately
  • Have first aid available
  • Control access points

Emergency Preparedness

Emergency Contact Numbers:

  • UCF Police Emergency: 911 (from campus phone) or 407-823-5555
  • UCF Police Non-Emergency: 407-823-5555
  • UCF Alert: Sign up for emergency notifications at my.ucf.edu
  • Environmental Health & Safety: 407-823-6300

Emergency Plan Checklist

  • Identify all emergency exits and evacuation routes
  • Designate assembly areas outside the venue
  • Assign staff roles in emergency situations
  • Know location of AEDs (automated external defibrillators)
  • Have first aid kits accessible
  • Create communication plan to notify attendees
  • Coordinate with venue staff on their emergency procedures
  • Brief all event staff on emergency protocols

Medical & First Aid

When to Have Medical Staff

  • Large outdoor events (500+ attendees)
  • Sporting events or physical activities
  • Events in hot weather or extreme conditions
  • Events serving alcohol
  • Multi-day conferences
  • Events with elderly or high-risk attendees

Basic First Aid Requirements

  • Have at least one staff member with first aid/CPR certification
  • Stock first aid kit with basic supplies
  • Know location of nearest AED
  • Have clear path for emergency vehicle access
  • Post emergency numbers visibly
  • Designate a quiet space for medical situations
