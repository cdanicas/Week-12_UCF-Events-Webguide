Arranging Hotel Room Blocks

When to Arrange a Room Block

  • Events with 10+ out-of-town attendees
  • Multi-day conferences or workshops
  • Events during busy tourism seasons
  • When offering convenience to attendees
  • For events requiring early arrival/late departure

Room Block Benefits

  • Discounted group rates (typically 10-20% off)
  • Guaranteed availability during busy periods
  • Simplified booking for attendees
  • Possible complimentary rooms for organizers
  • Meeting space may be included
  • Single billing option for organizations

How to Arrange a Room Block

  1. Contact hotel sales department 3-6 months ahead
  2. Provide event details and estimated room count
  3. Negotiate group rate and cutoff date
  4. Review contract terms carefully
  5. Get unique booking code or link for attendees
  6. Promote booking information to attendees
  7. Monitor room pickup and adjust if needed

Important Contract Terms

  • Cutoff Date: When block closes (typically 3-4 weeks before)
  • Attrition: Minimum rooms you must fill (usually 80%)
  • Comp Rooms: Free rooms based on pickup (often 1 per 50)
  • Cancellation Policy: Individual and group cancellation terms
  • Rate Lock: Ensure rate won't increase

Communicating Lodging Information

Include in Your Event Communications:

  • Hotel Block Details: Hotel name, address, group rate, booking code/link
  • Booking Deadline: Clear cutoff date for group rate
  • Alternative Options: List 2-3 other nearby hotels
  • Distance Info: Miles and driving time to event venue
  • Transportation: Parking info, shuttle services, rideshare options
  • Amenities: Breakfast, parking fees, WiFi, airport shuttle
  • Area Information: Nearby restaurants, attractions, safety tips

Lodging Planning Tips

  • Book Early: Especially during UCF graduation, Homecoming, or theme park peak seasons
  • Consider Airport Proximity: Orlando International Airport is 20-30 minutes from most campuses
  • Check Hotel Policies: Parking fees, pet policies, smoking rooms
  • Visit Properties: Tour potential hotels before committing to room blocks
  • Get Everything in Writing: All negotiated rates and terms should be in contract
  • Provide Alternatives: Some attendees may prefer Airbnb or other options
  • Consider Budget Range: Offer hotel options at different price points
