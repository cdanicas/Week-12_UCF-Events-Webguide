Conflict Management Best Practices

Important Considerations:

  • Check Multiple Calendars: Review all relevant calendars (campus events, athletics, academic) before finalizing your date
  • Plan Ahead: Book your event date at least 6-8 weeks in advance to secure venues and avoid conflicts
  • Consider Academic Calendar: Avoid scheduling during midterms, finals, breaks, and major university holidays
  • Athletic Events: Home football games draw 40,000+ attendees - plan accordingly for parking and traffic
  • Major University Events: Homecoming, graduation, and orientation weeks have limited venue availability
  • Weather Considerations: Florida weather can be unpredictable - have backup plans for outdoor events

What To Do If You Find a Conflict

  1. Assess the Impact: Determine if the conflicting event will significantly affect attendance or resources
  2. Consider Alternative Dates: Check if moving your event by a few days or weeks resolves the issue
  3. Evaluate Different Times: Morning events may not conflict with evening athletic games
  4. Choose Alternative Venues: UCF has multiple campuses - consider Lake Nona, Downtown, or Rosen if Main Campus is busy
  5. Coordinate with Other Organizers: Sometimes events can complement each other rather than compete
  6. Communicate Early: Notify attendees about potential parking or traffic challenges
