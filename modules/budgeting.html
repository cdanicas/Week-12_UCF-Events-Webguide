Plan your event budget effectively with our interactive calculator and best practices guide.

Event Budgeting Best Practices

Planning Ahead

  • Start budget planning 3-6 months before your event
  • Include a 10-15% contingency for unexpected costs
  • Get multiple quotes for major expenses
  • Track all expenses in real-time

Cost-Saving Tips

  • Book UCF venues early for better rates
  • Consider student organization discounts
  • Use UCF catering services when possible
  • Leverage free marketing through UCF channels

Common Budget Categories

  • Venue rental and setup
  • Food and beverage
  • Marketing and promotion
  • Audio/visual equipment
  • Entertainment or speakers
  • Decorations and supplies
  • Security and permits
  • Staff and volunteers

Tracking & Reporting

  • Save all receipts and invoices
  • Use the budget calculator to track spending
  • Export reports for stakeholders
  • Review budget vs. actual costs post-event

Funding Sources for UCF Events

Available Funding Options:

  • Student Government Association (SGA) - Funding for student organization events
  • Departmental Budgets - Contact your department's business office
  • Sponsorships - Local businesses and corporate partners
  • Registration Fees - For conferences or ticketed events
  • Grants - University and external grant opportunities
