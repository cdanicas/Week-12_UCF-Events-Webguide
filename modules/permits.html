UCF SAFE Form - Special Activity & Facility Event

Submit Your Event for Review

The SAFE Form is the university's process for reviewing and approving special events to ensure they meet safety, insurance, and regulatory requirements.

When Required:

  • Events with 50+ attendees
  • Events with external vendors or contractors
  • Events with inflatables, amusement devices, or special equipment
  • Events serving alcohol
  • Outdoor events requiring tents, stages, or structures
  • Events with inherent risk (sports, physical activities)
  • Any event requiring insurance certificates

Submission Deadline: At least 30 days before your event (60 days for complex events)

Event Permit Requirements Checklist

Do You Need a SAFE Form? Check if your event includes:

✓ Event Characteristics:

  • 50 or more attendees
  • Off-campus location
  • Serves alcoholic beverages
  • Involves minors (under 18)
  • Overnight or multi-day event
  • International travel

✓ Equipment & Activities:

  • Tents, stages, or temporary structures
  • Inflatables or amusement devices
  • Pyrotechnics or special effects
  • Athletic/sporting activities
  • Swimming or water activities
  • External vendors or contractors

SAFE Form Submission Process

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Gather Information: Event details, vendor info, insurance certificates
  2. Submit SAFE Form: Complete online form at risk.ucf.edu
  3. Await Review: Risk Management will review within 5-7 business days
  4. Address Feedback: Respond to any questions or required changes
  5. Receive Approval: Get written approval before proceeding
  6. Meet Requirements: Fulfill any conditions (insurance, waivers, etc.)
  7. Proceed with Event: Only after receiving final approval

What You'll Need to Provide

  • Event name, date, time, location
  • Expected attendance
  • Detailed event description and activities
  • List of all vendors and contractors
  • Vendor insurance certificates
  • Equipment rental agreements
  • Participant waivers (if applicable)
  • Safety plan and emergency procedures
  • Proof of permits from other agencies (if needed)

Insurance & Liability

Vendor Insurance Requirements:

All external vendors must provide certificates of insurance showing:

  • General Liability: Minimum $1,000,000 per occurrence
  • UCF Named as Additional Insured: Required on all policies
  • Workers' Compensation: If vendor has employees
  • Auto Liability: If vendor will drive on campus

Submit insurance certificates with your SAFE Form at least 30 days before the event.

Other Permits You May Need

Alcohol Permits

Events serving alcohol require:

  • SAFE Form approval
  • State alcohol license/permit
  • Certified bartenders
  • Security presence
  • ID checking procedures

Fire Safety

Large events may need:

  • Fire marshal inspection
  • Tent fire certificates
  • Cooking equipment approval
  • Evacuation plan
  • Fire extinguishers

City/County Permits

Off-campus events may require:

  • City special event permit
  • Noise permit
  • Road closure permit
  • Park use permit
  • Food service permit

Permit Planning Timeline

Event Planning Timeline

60+ Days Before Event

  • Begin gathering vendor information and insurance certificates
  • Review SAFE Form requirements
  • Identify any special permits needed

30-45 Days Before Event

  • Submit SAFE Form to Risk Management
  • Apply for any city/county permits
  • Ensure all vendors submit insurance certificates

14-30 Days Before Event

  • Receive SAFE Form approval
  • Address any conditions or requirements
  • Finalize safety plans

7-14 Days Before Event

  • Confirm all permits are in place
  • Brief staff on safety procedures
  • Prepare required documentation for event day
